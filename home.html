Welcome to NutriPulse AI (Day 1)

NutriPulse AI is a smart web application that helps you understand how your daily nutrition and lifestyle choices impact long-term health. This interactive demo (Day 1) focuses on local logging, simple analysis, and an approachable chatbot for basic wellness guidance.

Core features demonstrated

  • Log meals, water intake, sleep, and activity—all stored locally in your browser.
  • A simple health dashboard with a heuristic nutrition score, gut health status, and basic risk indicators for obesity and diabetes.
  • An integrated rule-based AI chatbot that answers common nutrition and wellness questions.

How it works

The demo runs entirely in the browser. Data is saved to localStorage, and a set of lightweight heuristics compute the dashboard values. This is a Day 1 prototype—intended to show interactions, UX, and simple analysis before connecting to a backend and real AI models.

Tech & next steps

Planned full app stack: a Python backend and MongoDB for storage, with AI/analytics components to provide personalized recommendations and risk predictions. Next steps after Day 1 include server integration, improved analytics, and richer chatbot experience.
